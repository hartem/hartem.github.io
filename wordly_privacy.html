Privacy Policy

App: Wordly  |  Last Updated: January 10, 2026

1. Introduction

This policy explains how Bluebear Technologies Ltd. ("we") collects and uses data in the Wordly mobile application. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

2. Data We Collect

We collect data to provide the game, improve performance, and serve advertisements. This data corresponds to our App Store Privacy disclosures:

  • Data Linked to You (Used for Tracking & Ads):
    • Identifiers: Device ID (e.g., IDFA, AAID).
    • Usage Data: Advertising Data.
  • Data Not Linked to You (Analytics & Functionality):
    • Usage Data: Product Interaction (gameplay actions).
    • Diagnostics: Crash Data and Performance Data.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • App Functionality: To run the game and save your progress.
  • Analytics: To understand how users play and fix crashes (using anonymous identifiers).
  • Advertising: To show advertisements that allow us to offer the app for free.

4. Third-Party Advertising (AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to serve ads. Google may collect and use your device identifier and advertising data to show personalized ads.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Tracking & iOS Permission (ATT)

On iOS devices, we request permission to track your activity across other companies' apps and websites to deliver personalized ads (App Tracking Transparency).

  • If you allow tracking: You will see ads more relevant to your interests.
  • If you decline tracking: You will still see ads, but they will be less relevant and not based on your unique identifier.

6. Children

Wordly is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your region). If we discover such data has been collected, we will delete it.

7. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your location, you have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Opt-out of targeted advertising via your device settings.

To exercise these rights, please contact us.
